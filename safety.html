Safety Guidelines

Naija Local connects buyers and sellers but does not hold money or guarantee any transaction. A few habits keep you safe.

Before you pay

  • Verify the product first. See it, test it and check it matches the photos.
  • Never send money to a seller you have not verified. Be wary of anyone who asks for payment before you have seen the item.
  • Inspect goods before payment. Phones, laptops and vehicles deserve extra checks, including documents.
  • Be cautious of unusually cheap offers. If a price looks too good to be true, it usually is.

When you meet

  • Meet in a safe public place, in daylight where possible, such as a busy shop, bank or filling station.
  • Tell a friend or family member where you are going and who you are meeting.
  • For large items, bring someone with you.
  • Do not share your home address, bank PIN, card details or one-time passwords with anyone.

For sellers

  • Use an area or town as your public location, not your house address.
  • Confirm payment has actually reached your account before handing over goods. Fake transfer alerts exist.
  • Mark a product as sold only when the deal is complete.

See something wrong?

Use the Report button on any listing. Our team reviews reports and can suspend listings and accounts. If you have been defrauded, also report it to the Nigeria Police Force.
