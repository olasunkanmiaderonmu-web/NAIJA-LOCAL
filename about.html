About Naija Local

Your neighbourhood market, now online.

Naija Local helps Nigerians buy and sell products close to where they live. Instead of scrolling through listings from across the country, you see what people around you are selling, in Naira, with the distance shown in kilometres.

What makes us different

  • Local first. Search starts within 55 km of you, so you can inspect goods and collect them yourself.
  • Talk directly. Contact sellers on WhatsApp or by email. No middlemen and no forced checkout.
  • Negotiate like a real market. Make an offer and agree a price with the seller.
  • One account. Buy and sell with the same account.

Important to know

Naija Local is a place to find, advertise and negotiate. Buyers and sellers agree prices and payment between themselves. We do not guarantee any transaction, so please read our Safety Guidelines before you meet or pay anyone.
