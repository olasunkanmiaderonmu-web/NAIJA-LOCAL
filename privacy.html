Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Naija Local ("we") respects your privacy and handles personal data in line with Nigerian data protection law. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, phone number, state, LGA, address and an optional profile picture.
  • Listings: product details, photos, price, the pickup location you choose and the contact numbers you choose to show.
  • Location: if you allow it, your device location to show products near you. It is stored in your browser, and only saved to your account if you choose to.
  • Activity: favourites, offers, enquiries, reports and product views.

What other people can see

  • Your name, profile picture and state appear on your seller profile and listings.
  • Your WhatsApp number, phone and email are shown on a listing only if you add them to it.
  • Your home or business address and your exact pickup pin are never shown publicly. Buyers see only the area you type and the distance.

How we use it

To run your account, show listings, calculate distance, send account emails (verification and password reset), prevent fraud and enforce our Terms. We do not sell your personal data.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with our infrastructure provider, Supabase, with access rules that stop users reading each other's private details. Photos are stored in secure file storage.

Your choices

  • You can edit your details and remove your listings at any time.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data by contacting us.
  • You can stop sharing your location in your browser settings.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Use our Contact page.
